Show how long each stage took on the job page

Today the stage table on the job page has the columns stage, kind, status, cost and share, and no time information. The human wants a duration per stage, such as "2m 14s", computed from the stage's started_at to its ended_at, shown as a new column after share.

Rules:
1. Each stage row gets a sixth cell after the share cell, holding the duration text. The header row gets a matching sixth th with text "duration" (inside the sr-only thead).
2. The duration is ended_at minus started_at, in whole seconds (fractions dropped). Examples: started_at 10:00:00, ended_at 10:02:14 shows "2m 14s"; 10:00:00 to 10:00:45 shows "45s"; 10:00:00 to 11:01:01 shows "1h 1m 1s"; 10:00:00 to 10:00:00 shows "0s".
3. Under one hour: "Nm Ss" when minutes are at least 1, otherwise "Ns". At one hour or more: "Hh Mm Ss". Seconds and minutes are never padded with zero.
4. A stage with no started_at or no ended_at (for example a stage still running) shows "-" in its duration cell.
5. The sum row gets an empty sixth cell, as the other sum cells are empty.
6. The costliest-row class, the share, the cost and the mismatch warning do not change.

Nothing else changes: the stage order, the cost and share texts, the "no stages" text, the decisions table and the timeline classes stay as they are.

Tests: build a job whose stages_detail has three stages with started_at and ended_at as in rule 2 and one stage with ended_at null. Check the duration cell texts are "2m 14s", "45s", "1h 1m 1s" and "-", the header has "duration", and the sum row's sixth cell is empty. Check the existing cell texts for the first five cells are unchanged.
