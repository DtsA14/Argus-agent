Add a duration column to the stages table of the job page

The stages table of the job page shows the name, kind, status, cost and share of every stage, but not how long a stage took. The human wants the duration of every stage, from its `started_at` to its `ended_at`, shown in a new column, for example `2m 14s`.

Rules:
- A new column goes between `cost` and `share`. The head of the stages table keeps its hidden row and its column headers become, in order: `stage`, `kind`, `status`, `cost`, `duration`, `share`.
- Every stage row gets one more cell, the duration, in that position. The first cell of a row is still the stage name, the fourth is still `$` plus `cost_usd`, and the last is still the share.
- The duration is the time from the stage's `started_at` to its `ended_at`, both ISO timestamps, in whole seconds, rounded down (a part of a second is dropped). 134 seconds shows `2m 14s`, 45 seconds shows `45s`, 0 seconds shows `0s`, 180 seconds shows `3m 0s`, 3600 seconds shows `1h 0m 0s`, 3734 seconds shows `1h 2m 14s`, and 86399 seconds shows `23h 59m 59s`. The hours part appears only from one hour on, the minutes part only from one minute on; the seconds part is always there.
- A stage whose `started_at` or `ended_at` is missing, `null` or not a text shows `-` in its duration cell. A stage whose `ended_at` is earlier than its `started_at` also shows `-`.
- Both fields are optional in the answer of `/api/jobs/<id>`: a stage object without `started_at` and without `ended_at` is a stage of the page, it shows `-` and never makes the page say `could not load`. A value that is neither `null` nor a text counts as missing and shows `-`.
- The sum row in the foot of the table keeps its first cell `sum`, its `$` money in the fourth cell, and gets an empty duration cell, so the sum row has six cells: `sum`, empty, empty, the money, empty, empty.
- The durations are the only new thing on the page: no new request is made and nothing is stored in the browser. A stage name that holds markup is still shown as its own text in the duration column too, so a stage named `<img src=x onerror=alert(1)>` adds no `img` element.
- The mobile timeline is unchanged: the body and the foot of the stages table keep the classes they have now, and a duration cell carries no class of its own.

Nothing else changes: the job heading, the `state, $cost, created …` line, the WIB line, the share column and its `-` for a job that cost nothing, the mark on the costliest stage, the `no stages` text, the `stage costs do not match the job cost` line, the decisions table and the phone timeline all stay as they are.

Tests: in a new file `src/job-page-stage-duration.test.jsx`, with a job whose three stages have `started_at` `2025-04-01T08:00:00Z` / `ended_at` `2025-04-01T08:02:14Z`, `2025-04-01T08:02:14Z` / `2025-04-01T08:02:59Z`, and `2025-04-01T08:02:59Z` / `2025-04-01T09:02:59Z`: the column headers are `['stage','kind','status','cost','duration','share']` and the duration cells are `['2m 14s', '45s', '1h 0m 0s']`; durations of 0, 180 and 86399 seconds show `0s`, `3m 0s` and `23h 59m 59s`, and 3734 seconds shows `1h 2m 14s`; a stage with `started_at: null` and a stage without the two fields at all show `-` and the page does not say `could not load`; a stage with `started_at` `2025-04-01T08:02:14Z` and `ended_at` `2025-04-01T08:00:00Z` shows `-`; the sum row has six cells and its fourth cell is `$0.0800` for stages that add up to 80000000 nano dollars; a stage named `<img src=x onerror=alert(1)>` shows that text in its first cell, adds no `img` element, and its duration cell shows its duration; reading the durations costs one request to `/api/jobs/j1` and leaves `localStorage` and `sessionStorage` empty.
