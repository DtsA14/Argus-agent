Add a duration column to the stages table of the job page

Today each stage row of the job page shows the stage name, its kind, its status, its cost and its share of the job cost; nothing says how long the stage took. The human wants each stage to also show its duration, taken from `started_at` until `ended_at`, for example `2m 14s`.

- A stage carries two new fields, `started_at` and `ended_at`: each is either a timestamp text (ISO 8601, as `created_at` is) or `null`. Both are optional in the api answer: a stage without them, or with `null`, still loads and shows `-`.
- The stages table gets one more column after `share`, headed `duration`: the header row is now `stage`, `kind`, `status`, `cost`, `share`, `duration`. The head stays visually hidden exactly as today.
- Each stage row gets one more cell at the end holding the duration text. A stage with `started_at: "2025-04-01T08:00:00Z"` and `ended_at: "2025-04-01T08:02:14Z"` shows `2m 14s`; the same instants written as `2025-04-01T15:00:00+07:00` and `2025-04-01T15:02:14+07:00` show `2m 14s` too.
- The text of the duration, from the whole seconds between the two instants (a fraction of a second is dropped, never rounded up):
  - under one minute, the seconds only: `0s` for the same instant twice, `1s`, `45s`, `59s`;
  - from one minute up to one hour, minutes and seconds, both always shown: `1m 0s` for 60 seconds, `2m 14s` for 134 seconds, `59m 59s` for 3599 seconds;
  - one hour and more, hours, minutes and seconds, all three always shown: `1h 0m 0s` for 3600 seconds, `1h 1m 5s` for 3665 seconds, `26h 3m 9s` for 93789 seconds.
- `-` (a plain hyphen, no spaces) is shown instead when the duration is not known: `started_at` missing or `null`, `ended_at` missing or `null`, a timestamp that cannot be parsed, or an `ended_at` earlier than its `started_at`.
- A timestamp that is present but is neither text nor `null` (for example a number) makes the page show `could not load: …` exactly as any other malformed stage does today.
- The new cell carries no class, like every other cell of the stages table: the `costliest` class stays on the row alone, and the timeline classes of the body and the foot stay as they are.
- The `sum` row in the foot gets one more, empty cell at the end, so it has six cells: `sum`, ``, ``, `$0.0800`, ``, `` for a job whose stages add up to `$0.0800`.
- The duration costs no request and is stored nowhere: the page still fetches only `/api/jobs/<id>`, and `localStorage` and `sessionStorage` stay empty.
- A stage name holding markup (`<img src=x onerror=alert(1)>`) is still its own text, and the page adds no `img` element.

Nothing else changes: the job heading, the summary line, the `created …` line, the stage order, the texts of name, kind, status, cost and share, the `costliest` mark and its stylesheet rules, the `sum` money, the `stage costs do not match the job cost` line, the decisions table and the `no stages` text stay exactly as they are.
