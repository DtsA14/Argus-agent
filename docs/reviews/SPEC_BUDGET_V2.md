# SPEC_BUDGET_V2 (F47 phase B, 2026-11-28): a daily and a monthly budget of actual spend

Strict lane. Status: for Opus review, read-only. Replaces "money, not time" and the topic pool (F48) of `docs/PLAN_FOREMAN_TOPICS.md`. Atha 2026-11-28: "kayak limit claude ... daily limit dan monthly limit": calendar days and months; $1 per day, $30 per month; at the limit a spending action is refused, the foreman's turns included.

## 1. Why

Today the daily cap (`chat.daily_cap_usd` 7.00, `_exposure_scan`) books each job's MAXIMUM for 24 hours from its creation: over all jobs made through the UI $18.80 was booked and $0.82 spent (4.4%); in the last 24 hours $3.90 booked, $0.28 spent. The booking also needs a session to die before 24 hours and cannot see a job older than 2 days. A limit on actual spend (like Claude's) is truer and removes both problems.

## 2. Definitions

- **Day** and **month**: calendar, in `Asia/Jakarta` (fixed UTC+7, no DST): the day resets at 00:00 WIB, the month on the 1st at 00:00 WIB.
- **Spend** of a window: the sum of `usd_nano` of the `llm_call` events (the charged amount: the larger of the harness count and the gateway's reported cost, as the ledger already holds it) whose `ts` is in the window, over EVERY stage ledger `runs/*/events.jsonl` of the jobs directory, whoever started the job (the chat or the CLI): one wallet.
- **Not counted** (said on the meter): runs outside the jobs directory (`route-eval`, `patch-trial`, `t3b-run`, pilots, a run with another `--jobs-dir`). The provider's dashboard is the wallet's truth; the meter is the jobs directory's.
- **Unknown**: a ledger that cannot be read, a `llm_call_started` without its `llm_call` (`usd_complete` false) or an amount that is not a finite non negative number makes the window's spend UNKNOWN, never zero. Unknown refuses every spending action (fail closed) and the meter says so.

## 3. Part 1, the meters (read only; built first)

1. `router/budget.py`: `Spend` reads the ledgers incrementally: a map `ledger path -> (size, mtime_ns, {WIB date: nano})`, re-read only when size or mtime changed (a ledger is append only; a shrunk one is re-read whole); lines that are not events are ignored, a line that cannot be parsed makes the file unknown. Memory only plus an optional rebuildable cache file in the chat's own directory (no authority in it, never trusted over the ledgers). Measured on this host: 635 ledgers, 16.4 MB; a cold scan must stay under 2 s (a test with 600 synthetic ledgers), a warm one under 50 ms.
2. Config `chat.budget: {daily_usd: 1.00, monthly_usd: 30.00, warn_at: 0.8}` validated in one place (finite, positive, daily at most monthly, `warn_at` in (0, 1]); `chat.daily_cap_usd` stays in this part and is still the gate.
3. `GET /api/budget` (read only, like the other reads): `{day: {date, spent_usd, limit_usd, resets_at}, month: {...}, unknown: bool, note: "jobs directory only"}` or `unknown` with a fixed reason.
4. Page: a chip in the header ("Today $0.28 of $1.00") and a section in the Cost tab (both meters, 80% in the warning colour, text only), refreshed with the existing poll.
Part 1 gates nothing and changes no cap: it shows the truth so Atha can compare it with the dashboard before part 2 relies on it.

## 4. Part 2, the gate (after part 1 is live and the meters have been compared with the dashboard)

1. **Admission.** Every action that can spend (`issue_new`, `issue_reply`, `issue_start`, `prd_new`, `prd_reply`, `research_new`, `manager_new`, the foreman's turn, `queue_go`/`queue_step`/`draft_approve`/`job_approve`, `patch_read_run`) must satisfy, for the day AND the month: `spend + reservations + reserve(card) <= limit`. `reserve(card)` is the cap the action authorizes for the stage it starts (the existing `exposure_of` amounts, per stage, not per job). `reservations` = for every stage RUNNING now (a job state that says a stage runs, or an in flight confirm the directory does not show yet, for at most 10 minutes) its stage cap minus what it has spent. Checked at prepare and at confirm (as `check_cap` is now). A foreman turn reserves its worst case (3 calls at `turn_cap_usd`).
2. **No booking.** `_exposure_scan` and its audit nets are removed from the gate (the 24 hour windows and the "created via the UI" log scans go; so does the 2 day name break). Actions that cost nothing (`prd_approve`, `code_apply`, `job_reject`, `job_abandon`) are never refused by the budget.
3. **Refusals** are one fixed sentence each: "the daily budget would be passed ($0.92 of $1.00 spent, this needs up to $0.15; it resets at 00:00 WIB)" and the monthly one; unknown: "the spend cannot be computed (reason)".
4. **Strict means the caps must fit the budget.** A guarantee that the day never passes $1 needs every stage cap far under $1. Observed maxima: writer job $0.023, draft $0.069, agent $0.125, readers about $0.01, research $0.105, a PRD turn $0.036. Proposed (Atha confirms, config only): `issue_writer.max_usd` 0.50 to 0.15, `issue_writer.turn.cap_usd` 0.10 to 0.05, `code_max_usd` 1.00 to 0.40, the patch readers' cap 0.20 to 0.08, research 1.00 to 0.30, PRD 1.50 to 0.30; the foreman's $0.20 session and $0.03 turn stay. The check that a stage cap cannot pass the job's remaining cap already exists; a test pins that no configured cap exceeds the daily limit.
5. **The foreman session** keeps its own cap ($0.20, one confirmation); its turns are admitted one by one against the budget (item 1), so its age no longer matters for money: `ttl_hours` is replaced by an idle limit (`idle_days`, default 7: a session with no turn for that long is BLOCKED), and the 23 hour wall of `settings` goes. Opening a new session when one ends is the topic work (phase C).
6. Removing `chat.daily_cap_usd`: the key is refused as unknown once part 2 lands; `waiting()`'s "capped" and "cap unknown" notes now speak of the budget.

## 5. Tests (deterministic)

Part 1: window edges at 00:00 WIB (an event at 16:59:59Z and at 17:00:00Z belong to different days; month edges, a leap day); a ledger with a call in two days; incremental re-read after an append; a shrunk ledger; unknown on a half written line, a started call without its end, a negative or NaN amount, an unreadable file; CLI and chat jobs both counted; the route and the chip text; warn colour at exactly `warn_at`; config validation; the 600 ledger timing bounds. Part 2: each action admitted at `limit - reserve` and refused one nano above, day and month separately; a running stage reserves its remaining cap and releases it when it ends; an in flight confirm reserves for 10 minutes only; unknown refuses spending actions and never the free ones; the foreman's turn refused at the limit with its fixed sentence; a stage cap above the daily limit is refused at config load; the removed key; reset times in the sentences.

## 6. Mutants (the reviewer extends; run once after the review)

A day boundary in UTC instead of WIB; month off by one; `ts` read as milliseconds; a started call counted as zero; unknown counted as zero; the cache not invalidated on append; a shrunk ledger not re-read; CLI jobs left out; the month meter showing the day; `warn_at` off by one; (part 2) admission with `<` instead of `<=`; the month not checked; reservations not released; a free action refused; the confirm check dropped; the foreman's turn exempt; the in flight reservation never expiring; the old exposure still consulted.

## 7. Not in this spec

Topics and continuing a topic (C), steps in the chat (D, E), the foreman's auto approval of a clean draft, and a pool of money per topic (not needed: the budget is the one door).

**A. Part 1 as built (replaces section 2 and 3.1).**
1. *Which ledgers:* exactly three patterns, no recursive search, no symlink followed (each file opened with `O_NOFOLLOW`, only regular files; a path that is a symlink or not a regular file is ignored and counted as `ignored`): `jobs/*/runs/*/events.jsonl`, `jobs/*/patch_read_ledger.jsonl` (the patch readers: 45 files today, not in `rj.spent_nano`), `jobs/env_propose_ledger.jsonl`. Fixture ledgers inside repo snapshots (`runs/R/source/...`, `runs/R/workspace/...`, `root/...`) are never listed.
2. *Which events:* `llm_call_started` and `llm_call` only (matching `harness.llm.reconcile_ledger`): a refused `llm_call` (`status == "refused"`) carries no money and is skipped; `reservation_restored` and every other kind are never summed. A call = one `call_id`: with its `llm_call` it counts that event's `usd_nano` at that event's `ts`; with only its `llm_call_started` it counts `worst_usd_nano` at the started event's `ts` (the harness charges it so: `unknown_after_crash`; 4 such calls exist on this host, 2 in November, and a call in flight is the same). Only a call whose amount (`usd_nano`, or `worst_usd_nano` when it has no end) is None or not a non negative integer makes the window containing its `ts` UNKNOWN.
3. *Reading:* incremental per file keyed by (inode, size, mtime_ns) with the offset of the last newline: only complete lines are read (bytes after the last newline are a write in progress and are ignored, not unknown); a line that does not parse is skipped (the harness's own `parse_lines` does the same, and a cut line from a crash is repaired by a newline, so it stays in the middle of the file for ever); a shrunk file or a changed inode is read again from the start. An unreadable file is UNKNOWN only if its mtime is at or after the start of the current month (a file that cannot even be stat'ed is UNKNOWN); an old unreadable ledger is ignored and listed in `ignored`. The meter never opens a ledger through `EventLog` (opening one writes a newline into it).
4. *No cache file.* Memory only. A cold scan runs at start (`Budget.warm()`, called before the server accepts requests), not inside the first request; a warm scan is cached for 5 seconds (`BUDGET_TTL_S`) and only ever runs inside a request handler (serial under `_SERIAL`) or the warm call.
5. *Time:* a fixed `timezone(timedelta(hours=7))`, not zoneinfo; day and month boundaries in that zone; spend and limits are integers of nano USD and compared as integers. The meter says "days in WIB; a provider's dashboard counts days in UTC, so they differ by up to 7 hours".
6. *Config:* `chat.budget: {daily_usd, monthly_usd, warn_at}` (finite, positive, daily at most monthly, `warn_at` in (0, 1]), unknown keys refused; absent means the meters are off (`GET /api/budget` answers `{"off": true}`), nothing else depends on it in part 1. `chat.daily_cap_usd` and the 24 hour booking are untouched.
7. *Output:* `GET /api/budget` returns `{day: {date, spent_usd, limit_usd, resets_at}, month: {...}, unknown: false}` or, when a window is unknown, that window's `spent_usd` is null and `unknown` carries `{reason, job}` (the first file's job id and a fixed reason word: "unreadable", "unpriced call"), plus `ignored: N` and `scanned: N`.
8. *Page:* a chip under the title (`Today $0.28 of $1.00`; unknown shows `Today ?` and the reason in the Cost tab; 80% and over in the warning colour) and a Cost tab section "Spent (daily and monthly budget)", text only; the old "Booked against the daily cap" section stays as it is.

## Rev 2 (2026-11-28, B1.1, after Atha compared the meter with the two dashboards: DeepSeek $5.69 against the ledgers' $5.74, OpenRouter $6.20 against the gateway-reported $6.10, the harness's upper bound $10.89). This section wins over Rev 1 where they differ. Atha: the limits ($1 a day, $30 a month) are for BOTH platforms together, shown grouped by platform; the spend outside the jobs directory is counted too, grouped by where it came from.

1. **What a call costs for the meter (its bill, not the upper bound).** `billed` = the event's `usd_reported_nano` when it is a non negative integer (the gateway's own cost: what the provider invoices), else its `usd_nano` (the harness's count, exact for a direct API such as DeepSeek). A started call without its end still counts `worst_usd_nano` (an upper bound for the few calls in flight or lost). Measured on this host since 1 November, de-duplicated: OpenRouter billed 6.096 against 6.20 (1.7%), DeepSeek 5.740 against 5.69 (0.9%). The per job caps keep using the harness's upper bound; only the meter changes.
2. **Platform of a call:** the prefix of the event's `model` before the first colon: `openrouter` shows as "OpenRouter", `deepseek` as "DeepSeek", `anthropic` as "Anthropic", any other prefix as its own text (title cased), no model as "Other". The platform is read from the `llm_call` event, or from the `llm_call_started` event for a started-only call.
3. **Sources.** `chat.budget.sources`: a list (at most 40) of `{label, path, layout}`; the chat's own jobs directory is always the first source, labelled "Argus jobs", and is not listed. `path` is absolute after expanding `~`; `layout` is `jobs` (the three patterns of Rev 1 A.1 under it) or `flat` (files named `events.jsonl` or `*ledger.jsonl` at most 3 directories deep under it, no directory named `.git`, `node_modules`, `fixtures`, `source`, `workspace` or `root`, no symlink). Labels are unique non empty text of at most 40 characters; a path that does not exist is listed as `missing` and is not unknown (a source that is gone has no spend to show); an unreadable file follows Rev 1 A.3 (unknown only if its mtime is in the current month).
4. **De-duplication.** A call is identified by `(call_id, ts rounded to the millisecond)`; a call seen in several sources or files counts once, for the first source in order (Argus jobs, then the config order). Copies of a jobs directory (a staging snapshot, a worktree's fixtures) are therefore harmless; they are still not listed.
5. **Output.** Each window gets `by_platform: {name: nano}` and `by_source: {label: nano}` (only the keys with spend; the sums equal the window's spent), the top level gets `sources: [{label, scanned, missing}]`. Unknown stays per window, with the source label in its `reason` object next to the job.
6. **Page.** The chip and the meters show the total of both platforms; under each meter two text lines "By platform: OpenRouter $0.15 · DeepSeek $0.07" and "By source: Argus jobs $0.15 · experiments $0.07" (text only, amounts rounded to cents, a group under $0.005 shown as "under $0.01"); the note says which sources are listed and that each platform's dashboard (days in UTC) is the truth for its own wallet.
7. **Conventions.** New experiments (route evals, trials, model comparisons) run their roots under `~/argus-experiments/` (one listed `flat` source), so they are counted without editing the config each time. `argus-staging` and the `wt-*` worktrees are not sources.
8. **Tests added:** billed = reported when present and `usd_nano` when absent, both in the same file; a reported value that is not a non negative integer falls back to `usd_nano`; a started-only call counts `worst_usd_nano`; platform from the prefix for each case above (including none and an unknown prefix); a call present in two sources counts once, for the first; the first source in config order wins; a `flat` source finds a ledger 3 deep and not 4 deep, skips `fixtures`, `source`, `workspace`, `root`, `.git`, `node_modules`, and a symlink; `layout: jobs` under a listed source; a missing source is `missing` and not unknown; the by-platform and by-source sums equal the spent; config validation (a duplicate label, a relative path, a bad layout, more than 40, a non text label); the lines of the page; a real-host check kept out of the test suite (a script that prints the table of this section for Atha's comparison).
9. **Mutants added:** reported ignored; reported used when it is not an integer; the platform of a started-only call taken from nothing; a call counted once per source; the config order ignored; `flat` too deep, or reading `fixtures` or `source`; a missing source made unknown; by-platform not summing to the total; the by-source line drawn as HTML.
